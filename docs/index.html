Cohort: BAT01 (Classes 2–8)

35 Core Habits (5×7 System)

Age-graded curriculum spanning Classes 2 to 8. Habits unlock across Starter, Builder, and Master age-tiers with 5x7 matrix weekly scoring.

  • Ages 7–9 (Starter): 5 foundational daily habits (1 per dimension)
  • Ages 10–11 (Builder): 15 active daily habits (3 per dimension)
  • Ages 12–14 (Leader / CEO): All 35 habits (7 per dimension)
  • 5 Growth Dimensions: Physical, Mental, Intellectual, Emotional, Financial
  • Scoring: 350-Point weekly score with Bronze, Silver, and Gold mastery tiers
Cohort: BAT02 (25-Week Curriculum)

30 Habits (25-Week Progression)

Workshop-driven progression starting with 5 Core Q Power Habits and adding 1 new dimension habit each week in a 5-dimension rotating cycle.

  • 5 Q Power Habits: Daily baseline (Eye Ball, Speed Reading, Concentration Music, Blind Fold, Affirmations)
  • 25-Week Curriculum: Unlocks +1 habit weekly in rotation: P → M → I → E → F
  • Week 1 (6 habits) → Week 25 (30 habits): Steady, non-congested compound growth
  • 6 Total Dimensions: Q Power Habits + Physical, Mental, Intellectual, Emotional, Financial
  • Week Selector: Jump to or review any of the 25 curriculum weeks on demand
