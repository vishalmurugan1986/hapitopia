This Privacy Policy applies to the Habitopia mobile application developed and published by Achariya Educational Trust ("Achariya", "we", "us", or "our").

Summary for Parents and Guardians: Habitopia is an educational habit development application designed for children ages 7 to 14. We respect child data privacy. All habit progress, profiles, logs, and reflection notes are stored 100% locally on your child's device. We do not transmit, collect, track, or sell any personal information to cloud servers or third parties.

1. Data Collection and Storage Practices

Habitopia adheres to strict privacy-by-design principles:

  • Local Only: Nicknames, ages (7–14), chosen avatars, habit completion ticks, scores (XP), milestone journals, and PIN codes are saved solely in the device’s internal sandboxed local storage (AsyncStorage).
  • No Account Creation: No email address, telephone number, physical address, biometric data, or government identity is required or collected.
  • No Server Sync: Habitopia operates entirely offline. There are no backend database servers collecting analytics, user logs, or tracking data.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

  • Zero Advertisements: Habitopia contains absolutely no third-party advertisements or ad-tracking SDKs.
  • No Analytics SDKs: We do not integrate third-party trackers, crash telemetries, or behavioral profiling tools.
  • No Location Tracking: The app does not access GPS, Bluetooth, camera, or microphone sensors.

3. Compliance with Child Privacy Laws

Habitopia strictly complies with global child privacy frameworks:

  • India’s DPDP Act 2023: Full adherence to children’s data protection rules under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. No profiling or tracking of children.
  • US COPPA: Compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • Apple & Google Families Policies: Meets all Apple App Store Kids Category and Google Play Families Policy guidelines.

4. Parental Control and Data Erasure

Parents retain complete sovereignty over all data stored in the app:

  • Pin Lock: A 4-digit PIN secures player profiles and prevents unauthorized modification.
  • Instant Reset: Parents can delete any profile and erase all stored logs and progress instantly by tapping "Reset Profile" under the Me/Settings tab.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the application from the device permanently purges all local data.
