How Habitopia Catches Pretended Completions
Every habit checked in the mobile app records its exact completion timestamp. If a student taps 3 or more habits in rapid succession (under 15 seconds) or marks morning routines in a single burst late at night, the system automatically flags their record with Review Needed: Rapid Tap. Teachers can click Spot-Check & Audit to view the exact second-by-second timeline and conduct a 30-second verbal spot check in class.
