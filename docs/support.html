Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Is Habitopia safe for young children?
Yes, 100%. Habitopia is completely ad-free, contains no third-party trackers, requires no email or phone login, and stores all child progress locally on the device in compliance with DPDP Act 2023 and COPPA.
2. What ages is Habitopia designed for?
Habitopia features age-graded progressions for children aged 7 to 14 (Classes 2 to 8), spanning Starter (5 habits), Builder (15 habits), and Leader/Future CEO (all 35 habits).
3. Can multiple children use Habitopia on the same device?
Yes! The Welcome screen features a multi-profile selector where siblings or students can switch between profiles, each protected by their secret 4-digit PIN.
4. What if my child forgets their 4-digit PIN?
Tap "Forgot PIN" on the login screen. The child can recover access by tapping their secret avatar chosen during profile creation.
5. Does Habitopia work without internet?
Yes. Habitopia operates entirely offline with zero network connectivity requirements.
