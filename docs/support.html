Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What are the 4-digit Batch Codes?
During player creation, children enter the confidential 4-digit Batch Code provided by their school administration to unlock their assigned curriculum (for BAT01 or BAT02 cohorts). Contact your school habit coordinator if you need your student access code.
2. How does the BAT02 25-Week Curriculum work?
BAT02 includes 5 daily Core Q Power Habits (Eye Ball Exercise, Speed Reading Card, Concentration Music, Blind Fold Practice, Power Affirmations) that students practice every day. In addition, 1 new dimension habit is unlocked each week in a 5-step rotating cycle (Physical → Mental → Intellectual → Emotional → Financial), scaling from 6 active habits in Week 1 to 30 active habits in Week 25.
3. How does habit proof / media evidence submission work?
When a child marks a daily habit complete, they are prompted to submit evidence (a photo, a short audio voice note, or a brief video clip). Once evidence is submitted, +10 XP is awarded and the completion timestamp is logged for school management review.
4. How do I turn Game Sound Effects on or off?
Game Sound Effects are enabled (ON) by default to provide encouraging audio feedback for daily habit clears. You can toggle sound effects on or off with a single tap using the speaker icon located on the main top header bar of the app, or from the Me tab under preferences.
5. Can multiple children use Habitopia on the same device?
Yes! The Welcome screen features a multi-profile selector where siblings or classmates can switch between profiles, each protected by their secret 4-digit PIN.
6. What if my child forgets their 4-digit PIN?
Tap "Forgot PIN" on the login screen. The child can recover access by tapping their secret avatar crest chosen during profile creation.
7. Does Habitopia work without internet?
Yes. Habitopia operates entirely offline with zero network connectivity requirements. All progress and media proofs remain safely on the device.
